# Version 1 — The Coma Cluster Distance Audit

Version 1 is preserved as the earlier stage of the project. It records the initial Coma Cluster distance audit before the methodology was refined in Version 2.

## Purpose

The project began from an interest in the expansion and age of the Universe, the Hubble law, and the Hubble tension. After reading about the Coma Cluster and examining NASA/NED astronomical data, the large spread of published distance measurements motivated a closer statistical investigation.

## What changed in Version 2

Version 1 led to several methodological questions: the listed measurements are heterogeneous, some records share references, and NED does not homogenize published distances onto a common zero point. Version 2 therefore treats Coma as a focused cluster-scale audit rather than treating every listed record as an independent, homogeneous observation.

Version 1 is kept as part of the project's development history rather than being presented as the final analysis.
